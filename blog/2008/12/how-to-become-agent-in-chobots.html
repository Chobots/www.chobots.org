How to Become an Agent in Chobots?



Q: What do Agents do?

Agents help. They help new chobots to learn about our chobot world. They help moderators to find and ban Nichos who use bad words or cheat. They help to develop Chobots world and spread news about it in their blogs and invite their friends. Agents should be regularly active in the game and know all the rules..

****

Q: What preferences do Agents have?

Agents have special blue chat, so that you can find an agent easily if you need help. They have an agent badge on their playercard. They have meetings with Moderators and help to make better the chobot world and recommend any chobots who they think also deserve to be an agent. Agents have their own graffiti wall to draw on and some special items to wear from a catalog in their own room called the HQ.

****

Q: How to become an Agent?

To become an agent you should be a regular player of the game and get to know how the game works. You should make a blog about chobots that tells others about the game and videos if you can. You should be polite and friendly at all times. You should follow the Chobot Rules at all times. You should help agents do their job, you can do this by helping the newcomers that join our game each day. Agents should also be smartly dressed. Be patient, it normally takes some time before you are able to become an agent. Don't ask to become an agent, you will be noticed if you help and play regularly.

****

Q: What happens if Agents break the rules?

They will be de-agented! Agents need to be role models to newcomers of the game and must uphold ALL the rules at ALL times. Agents need to still be seen helping others and playing regularly or they will lose their badge!

****

Q: What happens if an Agent gets hacked?

A hacked agent will be de-agented and will not regain their badge back. The only way to be hacked is if you have given out your pass and giving out your pass is against the rules.

****

Note: Anyone that is hoping to become an agent one day, should check that it is ok with their parents before making any blogs etc.
