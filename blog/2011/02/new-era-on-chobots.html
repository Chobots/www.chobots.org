New Era on Chobots :)

Hello chobots!

I have an awesome news for you! And for us, mods :)
New Era on Chobots starts today!!!
I suppose you guys have noticed that we didn't add much new stuff for past few months (year?). It's coz we were busy with other our projects. And now we're coming back to Chobots and for Chobots :)
From now on our artist will be adding more new cool items, backgrounds, magic items and (between us) I heard something about a new location :)
So guys! We need your help and ideas :)
Feel free to send us your ideas to our support email support@chobots.com
Me and Sapfira will really appreciate your interest in this matter :)
So design, draw, be creative with Chobots to make it better for us all!
