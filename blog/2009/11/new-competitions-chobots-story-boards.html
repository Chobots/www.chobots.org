New Competitions.... "Chobots Story Boards"

Hey Guys :)

I told you earlier in the week that there was going to be a new competition!
Well, Here it is! "Chobots Story Boards" :))
Only Six weeks till Christmas, so lets start getting in the spirit!!

This Story is titled "Merry's Chobot Christmas"
This Story board I have made is the beginning, the 1st page of the book!
It's a story about a Chobot called Merry and how she is going to enjoy her time
spent playing on Chobots at Christmas!!

(click the story board to view full size)

Now, here is what you need to do!!

Below is a blank board I have made for you to type your stories on
Click the blank board to see full size then right click and save it to your computer!

(click for full size, then right click and save to your computer)

Now you have your own blank board all ready to write your stories on!!

! I want you guys to continue the story of Merry enjoying her time spent on
Chobots at Christmas, make a paragraph or two, telling us what Merry saw or
did next, I would like you to be as imaginative and creative as you can be :))
Your finished board must also have a small drawing done by you at the side
of the story, which shows what is going on in your story!
Just like real picture story books do :))

SOME RULES :))
You must create your own stories of Merry and make your own drawings and
use the blank board I have provided, don't change the size!! otherwise your
entries cannot be accepted!!
Comment a link to your picture when you have finished, please check
yourselves that this link works and your story can be viewed full size!!

When all the boards are finished and put together, We will have made out very
own "Chobots Story Book" The 1st one, it will be Great!!
We will be able to have "Story Board" competitions for all kinds of events :))

You have some time to do this, so no need to rush!!

Now you remember the prize I mentioned, right??
Winners will receive the new Red Rosette :)) Plus Citizenship, Cool huh? :)))

Have fun making your "Story Boards"!!!
Any questions, I will answer in Comments

Jessie
