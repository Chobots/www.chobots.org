Riddles, Riddles

Good afternoon, Chos..  You are brillant!  Your answers were outstanding.  I had so much fun reading your ideas!

Updated:  Oops.   I updated the original post, and all 88 of your responses to the riddles copied over to this post.  Sorry about that..  But, hey...  You had terrific answers, and reading them again is kind of fun! 

1.  A man builds a house in a rectangular shape.  All sides have southern exposure.  A big, huge bear walks by the house.  What color is the bear?  Why?  Of course, the bear is a white polar bear.  The house is at the North Pole.

2.  How many birthdays does an average man have?  The average man is only born one (1) time even though he may celebrate this day every year..

3.  What unusual property do the words:  Flour, Tern and Thirsty   have in common?  Remove 1 letter from each word and you have:  Four, Ten and Thirty.

4.  A man is trapped on an island, surrounded by water and miles away from land.   He cannot swim.  Then suddenly one day he escapes.  He uses no tools.  How does he escape?  The water freezes and he walks to the nearest land mass.  (I love your ideas about hitching rides with dolphins and other sea life.  So very creative!)

5.  What type of phobia might a vampire have?  A vampire may have more than one phobia:  Alliumphobia--afraid of garlic and/or heliophobia--fear of sunlight.

Congrats to Heat2010 and MusicMaker25.

Strange Stone Decoded???

Wow.  You have some interesting and creative answers to what you thought this stone was.  Thank you for all your answers.

The stone is known as the Coligny Calendar, and was used around 200AD by people who spoke a combination of Latin and Gaulish languages. Most luni-solar calendars contained 355 days. Months with 30 days were marked "lucky" and months with 29 days were "unlucky". Every 2 1/2 years, the astronomers would add a 13th month to make the solar part match the lunar part.

Congrats to Shockz for correctly identifying the stone.

Strange Document Decoded


This document was created using the Elder Futhark Runic alphabet. This alphabet was used between 100CE and 700CE, and only contained 24 runes. There was no rune (letter) "C" in its alphabet, so you would have had to use the "K" to spell Chobots.

Atlantic Ocean Critters

You did wonderful answering the questions about Atlantic Ocean animals.  Here are the answers.

1. The blue whale is the largest existing animal. In Latin, it's name can means "muscular" or "little mouse", but there is nothing little about this animal!

2. The dolphin is a carnivore and closely related to the porpoise. The critter has very sensitive ears, hearing frequencies 10 times above human hearing. A playful animal, it loves to dance and entertain others. In order to avoid danger, the animal must swim continuously and only sleep for 4 to 60 seconds at a time.

3. North Atlantic puffins are often times mistakenly called penguins. They have cute little clown-like faces. They can dive up to 196 feet (60 meters) to catch fish to eat. They can fly up to 30 feet (10 meters) above the Ocean.

4. Octopuses and squids can change colors like a chameleon. When in danger, they can squirt clouds of ink. Ancient mariners thought these animals were sea monsters.

5. Manatees are often times confused with seals or sea lions, these animals are mammals that eat plants in the ocean, and seldom are seen on land. Located in the Caribbean and mid Atlantic area, these animals are very slow swimmers, and completely herbivorous. Their "sea cow" cousins lived in the Pacific Ocean, but are extinct now.

Congrats to Mtzbrave23 and Bouly.  Well, done..  Don't forget about the Sweet Battle Contest!
