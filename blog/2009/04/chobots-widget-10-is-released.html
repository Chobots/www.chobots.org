Chobots Widget 1.0 is Released!

Whohoo!

The first version of Chobots Widget is Released! Now you can track your favorite Chobots status and add it to your Blogs!

It looks like this!




To insert our widget to your blog, you need to copy paste this code and change 'login=' parameter twice to the chobot nickname you would like to track.

<object width="235" height="308"><param name="movie" value="http://www.chobots.com/game/CharWidget.swf?login=chlos&urlPrefix=http://www.chobots.com/game/"><embed src="http://www.chobots.com/game/CharWidget.swf?login=chlos&urlPrefix=http://www.chobots.com/game/" width="235" height="308"></embed></object>

I hope you're lucky to do it as I am! We will improve our widget constantly!
