kymaster said...

If choproff isn't on your screen. First off, he's on the server Vanilla at the Academy. But if hes not there, Do this.

IE Users:
Click on tools on the Menu Bar. Then click on Internet Options. Now click on the tab "General" and find Browsing History. Then click Delete. And select Temporary Internet Files. Then just log off then back on to Academy, Vanilla.

Firefox Users: Open up Firefox. Find the tools menu at the top of the browser. Select options, a new box will appear. Chose Privacy. Once open click on clear private data. Check the box that says cache. Click clear private data now. Give it a few moments to complete, then log off then log back on and go to Academy, Vanilla.

Hope I helped.
