Scrapbook Contest, Your Pet!!

Hey Guys :)

We haven't had a Scrapbook competition for a while so I've prepared a new one!!

This time I would like you to make a page about your Pet or your favorite Animal!!


Remember it's a Scrapbook page, not research and and I won't be looking for any text or pictures taken from the Internet!!

Click to view full size and then right click and save to your computer!

You must use the template provided, please don't change the size and please make sure your name is on your work!!

Comment working links to your finished page at this post or email it to me using the Scrapbook email, ChobotsScrapbooks@googlemail.com

The best of the best will receive the medal :)
