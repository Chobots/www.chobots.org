Chobots Olympic Games!

Hello Everyone!

There are a lot of fun games on chobots that you could play, and to make them even more fun and exciting, I came up an interesting schedule of events!

I'm talking about the new... Chobots.. OLYMPIC GAMES! :) These events/contests will be held every month, for 1 week.

Starting from Monday, there will be fun events throughout the day which will be held in this order:

Hide and Seek Mondays! (Monday - Hide and Seek)
Tug of War Tuesdays! (Tuesday - Tug of War)
Role Play Wednesdays! (Wednesday - Role Playing)
Tournament Tuesdays! (Thursday-Chess, Checkers, Sliders, Garbage Collector, every game!)
Musical Fridays! (Friday - Enjoying in-game songs)
Truth or Dare Saturdays! (Saturday - Truth or Dare)
Sweet Sundays! (Sunday - Sweetbattle)

Yay! I'm so excited! The first chobots Olympic Games!!! Every winner gets a prize!


-Tovictory
