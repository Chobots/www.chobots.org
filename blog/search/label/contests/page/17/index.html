Arctic Animals

The past week has been very busy, but I didn't forget about this contest..  Here are the answers to the Arctic questions:

 Arctic ground squirrels live in dens and are the only Arctic animal to hibernate.
 Lemmings look like furry, fat hamsters.
 A wolverines looks like a small bear, but is a member of the weasel family.
 Caribou are members of the deer family and are covered in a thick coat of hollow hair.
 Narwhals live in the Arctic Ocean, and have long ivory tasks.
 Cottongrass, as well as other plants, are used to make wicks for oil lamps.

Congrats to all of you for a job well done. I hope that you learned something about the Arctic. Sweetgirl25, Bobscowsgomoomoo, Choobaca55 and Vasa12345 taught me some new things. Thanks!
