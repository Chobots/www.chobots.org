Brain Pop #1

 
Luckily, we do have brains.   The brain is vital to most animals. Everything we do, say, eat, think, dream and remember is controlled by our brain! So, let's find out what the brain does.  To add to the fun, we will use our cerebrum to answer some riddles.

1.  How fast do brain messages travel?
2.  What part of the brain controls breathing?
3.  What part of the brain controls emotion?
4.  What part of the brain regulates your body's temperature?

Riddles:

5.  I'm in a place where weight does not exist, and every direction I face is up.  What am I?
6.  I'm made of stuff around me, but lighter than it.  More of me is hidden then see.  What am I?
7.  You use me to travel fast, but yet you never get far from the first place you passed.  What am I?

When you know the answers, please post them in a comment with your cho name. I can't wait to see you answers! 

After you are finished, travel to the Farm and help out Daisy, the Cow.
