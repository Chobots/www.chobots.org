Do you like my menu?

It's time for a little fun. Use your amazing design abilities and create a new Chobot Menu.  The menu may include any items you think that other chos would like to eat.  You can even create brand new foods if you want! 

Please make sure to add your cho name to the menu, and post the link to your creation in a comment.  Just thinking about your menu is making me hungry!
