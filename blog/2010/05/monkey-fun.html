Monkey Fun

Isn't the monkey cute? I wonder what he is thinking!?! Any ideas?

Let's find out about monkeys.. Here are five questions for you to ponder. Please write your answers in reply to this post. Winners will receive a prize.

1. What was the name of the 1st monkey that went into outer space?
2. There are 12 years in the Chinese zodiac calendar. What year is the Monkey?
3. In Japanese folklore, there is a story about 3 wise monkeys. What is the story called?
4. What is a group of monkeys called?
5. Monkeys can range from 3-5 ounces (85-140 grams) to 80 pounds (36 kilograms) in weight! What is the name of the smallest monkey?

Have fun learning about monkeys!

--Puffles
