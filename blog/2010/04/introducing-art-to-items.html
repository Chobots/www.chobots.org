Introducing: Art to Items!

Hello Chobots!
I'm Chobot. Most of you have probably heard of me but I am a moderator here at Chobots. I will now be posting here, with an exciting new segment named Art to Items! There's many of you who have a ton of Chobots item designs that you would like to see as an item in Chobots, so this will now be a way in which everyone can send their designs and have a chance for their design to become a Chobots item!! Here's how it will work:

I will post twice a week with one Chobots item design that someone has sent in (it won't be random, it will be ones that we like!). Then after 4 weeks, (a month) all of the 8 picked designs from that month will then be put onto a poll, and you guys will vote on which item you guys think should come to life and be an item in Chobots! And the artist who made the winning item that month, will receive their item! The winner's item will most likely be the membership item of the month! (1, 6, or 12 month)

So send all of your artwork to chobotsitems@gmail.com. So this means, you no longer send any Chobots item designs to the support email, you should now send it to chobotsitems@gmail.com! I will make a post on the 1st of May posting the very first item for that week. And also, your artwork isn't judged on how good it is drawn, but the idea of the item. So you do not have to be the best artist to get your item made, you just have to be creative! You can send in as many artwork designs as you want. (even stuff you have already made!)

Send em in!

-chobot
