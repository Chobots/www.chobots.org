Chobots Comic Contest #1 :)

Hey Guys :)

Are you ready to start making Chobot Comics??
I hope so as I have the 1st competition for you ^_^

Here is the Comic template that you MUST always use to make your Comics, it is the page where you will add your pictures (frames) to make the story of your Comic, your Comic can have more pages then just 1 but I wouldn't make it too long if I were you :)

Click to view full size (it's big) then right click and save it to your computer!



For your 1st Comic all I want you to do is make it "FUNNY" I know you guys
will be good at this :))

I have made a help section in a tab on my Jessie blog for these comics, all you need to know is written there, please read it, Click this link to go Chobot Comic Help

Also there is now a Blog made just for Comics http://chobotcomics.blogspot.com/
You can make Chobot Comics whenever you like and about whatever you like, just for fun and comment me a link on that blog then I can post some for all to read, you could also make your own blog just to show your talent in Comic making :)

You have some time to do this, so please don't rush the making of them as presentation is also going to count when deciding winners, I haven't decided what the prizes are going to be yet but it will be COOL :)

Comment working links to your Comics here!!

Have fun and enjoy making your Comics, I can't wait to start reading them :))

Jessie
