Hey Chobots!!

The questions are just for one server, if I have asked you to count something, then
you only have to count on 1 server, not all of the servers :D

what colors are they?

3.) In the cafe, if you buy everything in the menu on the table, how many
bugs is it going to cost you in total?

4.) How many bugs does it cost to buy the color shower for a Citizen house?

5.) In Chopix Adventures level 2, how many Cho-energy is there to collect
and how many accelerators?

6.) If you add up all the counters on all the sliders tables at the start of a game
how many counters are there in total?

7.) In Academy Tips, how old is the oldest wheel estimated to be?

8.) If you bought all the Magic in the Citizen magic catalog, how many bugs
would it add up to in total?

9.) There is a Brown Hat on display in the shop, tell me for what occasion the
hat was put into the catalog for?

10.) In the word "CHOBOTS" find a word that is something to wear.

Have fun finding the answers and comment them here!!
