Attention All Bloggers!!

Hey Guys :)

I was thinking that we have this item, the Feather which was made for blog competitions but not very many of you you own one at the moment!!

So, from now on, once a month, I will make a post and ask you if you think you and your blog is worthy of being awarded the feather item and I will check them to see!!


To earn it you will be expected to post very regularly, every day would be great, especially any updates or changes that is happening in Chobots!
Party announcements and pictures are cool and any fun stuff or information that you think will be interesting to read!

There is always something going on in Chobots, so this should be easy, You could also make all sorts of competitions for "fun" and although you can't give the winners an item prize, you could show their player card on your blog or have a little party with your friends for them maybe!

It should also be well presented to the best of your abilities :)

If you think your blog already meets this criteria then don't hesitate to comment me a link to it now and I will check it out! I will follow and keep my eye on them for a while ;)

Have fun on Chobots this evening :)
