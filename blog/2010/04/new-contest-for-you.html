New Contest For You

Hello there!

Today I got an email from Coffee2353 with an interesting contest idea!
Actually this contest is posted on Zarich's blog, but I'd like more people to know about it :)

So the point is, as Coffee2353 says:

You know how Google always decorates the words "google" on their homepage for certain Holidays? Well I want you guys to be super creative and do the same thing... but with the words "chobots"!

Use Chobot's underground wall or the paint program or anything you want to write the words "Chobots" in an earth day theme. Remember, you must write the WORDS not just draw a picture around them!

So are you in? I know it's a bit late for an Earth day but let's do it :)

Leave the working link to your picture in a comment to this post!
Winners get bugs!

Hiki & Coffee2353
