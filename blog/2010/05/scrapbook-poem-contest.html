Scrapbook Poem Contest!!

Hey Scrappers :)

Time for another Scrapbook contest!!

This time I would like you to make a Chobot Poem page!!
Make one half of the page the poem and the other a picture that is relevant to your poem!!

Click the page to view full size then right click and save to your computer!!

Make sure your Chobot name is on the page and plz don't change the size!
Comment working links to your finished poem at this post or email them to me using the Scrapbook email!!

You have a few days, have fun with this!! The best of the best will receive the Medal!!

Jessie
