A Cool and Comfortable Home

Awww.  Isn't this Harp Seal adorable? 

As you know, Hiki had a wonderful research contest about Angora Rabbits.  For some reason, the picture of the rabbit reminded me of the Harp Seal, which is a native of the Arctic Circle.  So, here are some questions about the Arctic Ocean and Arctic Circle.

1.  What Arctic animal lives in a den and is the only Arctic animal to hibernate?
2.  What Arctic animal looks like a furry, fat hamster?
3.  What Arctic animal looks like a small bear, but is a member of the weasel family?
4.  What Arctic animal is a member of the deer family and is covered in a thick coat of hollow hair?
5.  Which whale lives in the Arctic Ocean, and has long ivory tasks?
6.  The seed heads from which Arctic plant is used to make wicks for oil lamps?

Please place your answers in a comment, and include your cho name.  Winners will receive prizes.
