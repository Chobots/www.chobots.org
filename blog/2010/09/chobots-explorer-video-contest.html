The Chobots Explorer Video Contest!

Hi guys! William here,

Who likes going on adventures? Even little ones like a walk in the woods; I sure liked to!

So where are you going to explore?
Space? Underground? In the woods? Under your bed? D:

Prizes will be awarded for the best three videos.

Please post your video as a link in the description!

It would really help if you already have a camera / medals, if you could please comment saying what you have so I can build a list ;)

Don’t rush!
