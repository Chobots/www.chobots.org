Christmas Songs

Good afternoon, Peeps..  Winter is in the air, and Christmas is coming soon.

Do you have a favorite Christmas Song?  Maybe a favorite song about Winter?  There are so many to choose from, aren't there?

Here's your task:  Create a Christmas, or Winter, Song about Chobots!  You can use either the advert or blackboard template.  When finished, please post the link to your song in a comment with your cho-name.

You have one (1) week to write your song.  Who knows...  Maybe we will have Caroling at Cafe St this year!
