Chobots Scrapbooks!!

Hey Guys :)

Welcome to Chobots Scrapbooks!!


We will be using the pages of the Scrapbook to create all sorts of things for some fun competitions!

The "double page" of the book is nice and big for you be able to fit all kinds of things on it, Stories, Poems, Art, Research etc and here at this new blog Chobots Scrapbooks you will have a place to see your Scrapbook pages published!!

Notice the Medal that is seen on the front of the Scrapbook? Well that will be the main prize for good Scrapbooking (I don't think that's an actual word, but it sounds right :D) :))


Click to view full size (it's big) then right click and save to your computer

For the 1st competition and as this is the 1st page of "your" Scrapbook I want you to write something about your Chobot character, you can tell us about your favorite games, friends etc if you wish and include a small picture of yourself, that will take up one side of the pages and on the other side I want you to pick a subject that interests you that and you think may be of interest to others and write about it also including a small picture.
Basically it's a project about you and a topic of interest!!

Have fun with this and I will pick some winners to receive the Medal, in this case based on most interesting and nicely presented!!

Upload and comment working links to your finished pages at this post!

NOTE: Don't change the size and you must add your Chobot name to the page!!

Jessie
