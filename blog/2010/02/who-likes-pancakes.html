Who Likes Pancakes??

Hey Guys :)

Tomorrow in the Uk it is Pancake Day (yum) on this day people all over England will be eating Pancakes with all different kinds of toppings, personally I like mine with just lemon and sugar!!
English Pancakes are thinner then the American ones you normally see and are rolled up with the filling inside, just like in the picture!!

So, I would like you guys to draw me some Pancakes with your favorite filling and I will pick the yummiest ones to receive a prize!!



Remember, you must actually draw your pancake, I won't accept any pictures taken from the Internet!!

You don't have long, winners will be posted tomorrow, so get drawing!!
Comment your pictures here!!

Do you celebrate Pancake Day where you live? I would be interested to know!

Have fun - mmmm, I'm hungry now :))
