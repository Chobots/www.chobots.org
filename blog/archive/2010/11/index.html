Riddles, Riddles

Good afternoon, Chos..  You are brillant!  Your answers were outstanding.  I had so much fun reading your ideas!

Updated:  Oops.   I updated the original post, and all 88 of your responses to the riddles copied over to this post.  Sorry about that..  But, hey...  You had terrific answers, and reading them again is kind of fun! 

1.  A man builds a house in a rectangular shape.  All sides have southern exposure.  A big, huge bear walks by the house.  What color is the bear?  Why?  Of course, the bear is a white polar bear.  The house is at the North Pole.

2.  How many birthdays does an average man have?  The average man is only born one (1) time even though he may celebrate this day every year..

3.  What unusual property do the words:  Flour, Tern and Thirsty   have in common?  Remove 1 letter from each word and you have:  Four, Ten and Thirty.

4.  A man is trapped on an island, surrounded by water and miles away from land.   He cannot swim.  Then suddenly one day he escapes.  He uses no tools.  How does he escape?  The water freezes and he walks to the nearest land mass.  (I love your ideas about hitching rides with dolphins and other sea life.  So very creative!)

5.  What type of phobia might a vampire have?  A vampire may have more than one phobia:  Alliumphobia--afraid of garlic and/or heliophobia--fear of sunlight.

Congrats to Heat2010 and MusicMaker25.
