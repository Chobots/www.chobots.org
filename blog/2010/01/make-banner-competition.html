Make A Banner Competition!!

Hey Guys :)

I have a New Competition for you all!!

I would like you to make a Chobots Banner like I have here :)


Make something that will fit on the side of blogs, so no bigger then I have
done here and then people can use them on their blogs if they wish :)

Be original and use your own ideas for your banners, comment me a link to
your finished work here (please check that your link works!!).

The best of the bunch will be awarded the Red Rosette ^_^
You have a few days to do this, Have fun :))

NOTE: Guys, this isn't a competition to make a header, just a Chobot picture that
advertises Chobots that people can have on the side of their blogs if they wish!!
