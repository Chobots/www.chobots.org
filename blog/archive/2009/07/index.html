Cats, Dogs and other Pets ;)

Hello chobots!

Today I'd like to tell you some interesting info about your pets!
Let's start from cats!

The biggest breed of domesticated cats are called a Maine Coon cat and weighs up to 11 kg.
Cats' sense of smell is 14 times stronger than that of humans.
Cats have 230 bones in their bodies, this is 24 more than humans.
The print on a cat's nose is like that of a fingerprint of a human, each is unique.

It is a myth that dogs are color blind. They can actually see in color, just not as vividly as humans. It is akin to our vision at dusk.
Like human babies, Chihuahuas are born with a soft spot in their skull which closes with age.
The average city dog lives 3 years longer than a country dog
The U.S. has the highest dog population in the world
Newfoundlands are great swimmers because of their webbed feet
Basset Hounds cannot swim
Dog’s nose prints are as unique as a human’s finger prints and can be used to accurately identify them.

While eating, parrots hold their food in one of their feet, which can be either left or right, making parrots left as well as right handed.
Parrots, mainly pet ones, are very good at mimicking. They can easily imitate the speech and calls of other birds and animals, even humans. African gray parrots are the best mimics.
Larger parrots can live more than 75 years, while smaller ones live up to 15 years only.
Parrots cannot eat chocolate because it is poisonous to their body.


The native habitats of hamsters are the deserts of Asia.
Hamsters borrow their name from the German word ‘Hamstern’, which means to hoard.
Hamsters remember their relatives.
Hamsters are color blind.
Hamsters are normally allergic to Cedar.




I hope you liked the facts :)
See you at the party!!!!

Protect Yourself!

Hello guys!

I'm here to tell you very useful information! We're gonna talk about scammers and how to protect yourselves.
So I'd like to give you a few examples of scamming. ATTENTION! Don't let them cheat you!
First example. If you see someone saying "I can make you a citizen/agent/moderator!" Do NOT trust and do NOT give out your password!

Example two. Once when I was playing with my secret account one chobot told me: "Wow! Know what? I have 500 000 bugs! My friend knows a cheat! And I can tell it to you if you give me your pass!" (And know what? I didn't see that chobot anymore ;) Don't trust and DON'T tell your password!!!

Example three. If you see someone saying: "I can give you my member account on CP or smth but you have to give me your chobots password!" STOP! DON'T do that! Don't give out your password!

It was a short guide 'Protect yourself' :) Now you are armed and can recognize a typical scammer!

But let's talk about things you have to do if you were scammed. It's a pity but it's not the end of the world ;) Everything is under control!
So listen, if a scammer changes your pass and you can't log on - don't worry! All you have to do is restore your pass! It's simple :) Look:

After clicking 'Restore it' you see this:

Just type your email address you used during the registration and check your mail.
After you get an email with your current password you have to change it. Log on Chobots and click 'Menu' button and choose 'Change your password' option.

You have to change it to something different!
And you'll have your account back ;)
So if you follow these simple rules you'll be safe!
