Chobots Spring Blogs

Hey Guys :)

Time to announce the winners for the Spring Blog Contest!!

Blog by Smartdetective

Blog By Kiwii

Blog By Im_so_emo_9

Blog By Gelu_cool

Nice Blogs, well done guys you win the Feather :)

Some tips for the Bloggers out there!!
  • Post regularly, like everyday if possible, there's always something happening in Chobots to post about ^_^
  • Always post the updates and changes happening in Chobots.
  • Make them interesting to read and pleasing to the eye.
  • Check spellings and keep your blog looking neat, if a picture doesn't fit properly then try again and see if you can fix it.
  • Don't have a ton of trackers at the top of your blog, it makes your blog very laggy and your reader may leave before it's finished loading.
  • If you have rain on your blog, check to see that it doesn't interfere with people commenting, some of your blogs I tried to follow and couldn't because the rain stopped me from clicking on your followers.
  • If you want to become an Agent of Chobots then having a blog that's helpful and informative and regularly updated shows us that you take a good interest and are committed to the game.
