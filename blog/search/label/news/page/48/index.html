Protect Yourself!

Hello guys!

I'm here to tell you very useful information! We're gonna talk about scammers and how to protect yourselves.
So I'd like to give you a few examples of scamming. ATTENTION! Don't let them cheat you!
First example. If you see someone saying "I can make you a citizen/agent/moderator!" Do NOT trust and do NOT give out your password!

Example two. Once when I was playing with my secret account one chobot told me: "Wow! Know what? I have 500 000 bugs! My friend knows a cheat! And I can tell it to you if you give me your pass!" (And know what? I didn't see that chobot anymore ;) Don't trust and DON'T tell your password!!!

Example three. If you see someone saying: "I can give you my member account on CP or smth but you have to give me your chobots password!" STOP! DON'T do that! Don't give out your password!

It was a short guide 'Protect yourself' :) Now you are armed and can recognize a typical scammer!

But let's talk about things you have to do if you were scammed. It's a pity but it's not the end of the world ;) Everything is under control!
So listen, if a scammer changes your pass and you can't log on - don't worry! All you have to do is restore your pass! It's simple :) Look:

After clicking 'Restore it' you see this:

Just type your email address you used during the registration and check your mail.
After you get an email with your current password you have to change it. Log on Chobots and click 'Menu' button and choose 'Change your password' option.

You have to change it to something different!
And you'll have your account back ;)
So if you follow these simple rules you'll be safe!
