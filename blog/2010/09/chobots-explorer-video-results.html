Chobots Explorer Video Results!

Hi Explorers, William here,

I have the results for the Chobots Explorer contest, I have to say some of them were very cool ;)

We have a new system so if you are a runner up you get points towards a camera!

Also I would like you to read the new Chobots videos terms and conditions: http://chobotsvideosterms.tk

With our best video we have Coolguy432! His video was 100% epic! Expect more amazing videos from Coolguy432 ;) You win 2 weeks of citizenship and a V-flag


And now we are into our runners up!


 NOTE ABOUT THE POINT SYSTEM: Ok, it seems some of you are not understanding the new point system! Videos contests will be just as normal as before but with an added bonus. The point system is an EXTRA thing to give those who haven't won but who William thinks are making good efforts in their videos and participating in contests etc a chance to earn the camera by collecting points, points will vary between 1-3 and when 10 is reached you get a camera, this is a benefit to runners up, rather than just have your video link shown, you now get points towards the camera!!!!

Well done to Armycho got 1 point for his video! - http://youtu.be/s73Ylj8EucY

Also well done to 34sr who also got 1 point! - http://youtu.be/G_SpzknFYYU

To have a look at the points table go to: http://chobotsvideopoints.tk

Well done everyone and let’s see if we can get in the swing of this new system!

-William
