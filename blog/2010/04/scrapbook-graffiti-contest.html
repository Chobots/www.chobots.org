Scrapbook Graffiti Contest!

Hey Guys :)

Time to make another page for your Scrapbooks!!

I want you to make a Chobots Graffiti Page like I have made here just as a random example!

It's Graffiti, so make it LOUD, make it PROUD and make it NOISEY


Click to view full size

Take a copy of this Template
Click to view full size and then right click and save to your computer!

Make sure you Chobot name is on the page somewhere and plzzzzzz don't change the size!! Comment working links to your finished pages at this post or email them to the Scrapbook email ChobotsScrapbooks@googlemail.com!

Remember my page is just an example, use your own style and flair in your own page!
Think original!

You have until Monday to get them done, winners will receive the Medal or if you own it already you can win a V Flag in a color of your choice!

Get your pens, pencils, mouses :D to the ready!!
