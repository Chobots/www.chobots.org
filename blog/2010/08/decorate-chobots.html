Decorate Chobots!

Hey guys :)

Autumn is coming! I loooove this season, all the yellow leaves, rains and holidays + my B-Day =) It's awesome!
So I decided to make an interesting contest for you.
Please copy these pictures to your comp, pick the one you would like to decorate and start!

(plz open the pics to view full size)
I chose 3 the most popular locations: Cafe Street, Park and Academy Street :)
You can decorate them all, 2 of them or the one you like the most. It's up to you!
Now look at the place on the pic where your bugs are usually are: put your chobots name on there! Don't forget to do that plz :)
Ok, I hope you have fun doing that. Use all your imagination and creativity to decorate the locations. Remember the theme - AUTUMN =)
Good luck!
Winners get bugs and citizenship. You have one week to do that!
