Templates for your Poems

Hey guys =)

I'm going to post your poems soon, so I want you to make nice Halloween templates.

  • How to do that? Use Paint, Photoshop etc. The size of your template must be 500x600 px. Use your creativity to decorate the template.
  • Remember! I'll put a poem in the middle of your template! Don't make it messy! 
  • Why templates? Winners will get something cool as a prize! + It will be easier for me to post your poems that way. 2 in 1 contest :D
  • When? You have time till Friday!
  • Where to post? Post a link to your template in a comment to this post.

P.S. Make it spooky! Make it cool! =)
