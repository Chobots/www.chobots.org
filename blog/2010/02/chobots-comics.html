Chobots Comics!!

Hey Guys :)

I had an idea for a new competition and that's for you to all make Chobots Comics!!

I thought it would be fun for those that aren't able to make videos and for those that can of course. You will be able to make stories with pictures and put them onto the Comic pages to tell your story, like I have done here :)

(Click to enlarge)

This is just to give you the idea of what I am looking for in presentation of your Comics, when I make a competition I will give you a theme for your story and sometimes you can just make your own, there will be a blank template for you to use to put your pictures on, the same one I have used here and it can have more pages than just one :)

I will make a competition tomorrow, you can comment and give me some good ideas for what the 1st story should be for our Chobot Comics!

Do you like this idea??

Jessie :)
