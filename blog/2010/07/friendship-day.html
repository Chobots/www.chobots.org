Friendship Day

Hey there!

Do you guys know that this Sunday, the 1st of August we celebrate International Friendship day ?
I want you to design a card for your chobots friend!
Please use this template:

Right side - Your text. Left side - Drawing :)
Decorate it, make it bright and write your best wishes to your best friends!
You can print it out and draw using pencils and stuff or you can edit the card in Paint :)
Post a working link to your card in a comment to this post.
The best cards will be posted on the blog!
I can't wait till this Sunday!!! :)
