New Video Contest - Horror

Hey guys, William here!

I hope everyone is having a great or horrifying Halloween!

This week I am making a video contest following up from Halloween.
Make your own horror movie!

But there is one catch, somewhere in your video you have to include a kite! (Doesn't have to be this one)

It will be interesting who adapts this best into their video!

Prizes will be awarded for:
  • Best Video
  • Best Integration of the kite
  • Most Horrific! (In a good way ;)
You have 1 week to complete your videos (7th November 2010)

When finished please post your video link in the comments.

Don’t eat too much candy or sweets tonight!

-William
