Comic Contest Time!!

Hey Guys :)

Yep, time for another Comic contest me thinks!!

The subject this time will be about "Presents" :)

Everyone loves presents right?
It can be about receiving or giving one, a surprise present, whats inside, a big one, small one, you get the idea? anything about presents will do :)

Click to view full size and then right click and save to your computer!

Normal rules apply here, you must give your comic a title and put your chobot name on it, don't change the size, use more pages if needed and then comment a working link to it here!

If you need some help with your comic, then click this link COMIC HELP

TIP: If you can't read the writing on your comic frames and it distorts when you try to enlarge them, then use Chobots Big Screen when you are taking your pictures, It really helps!

Winners will receive the Comic item!

Have fun today :)

Jessie
