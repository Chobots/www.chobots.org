Moon Party Poem :)

Hello chobots:)

I found a really interesting poem! It's made by Yumega)

“Hey guys,” Hiki screamed
“Its party time!” we all beamed
We all got ready to start the show
But Hiki said, “Guys, where should we go?”
“What?!” We asked, what did she mean?
“Maybe we can all go, where all chobots dream?”
“Hiki, why must you fiddle?”
“Yeah! Instead of a party you give us a riddle!”
“Come on guys, it aint that hard!
Look inside! Follow your heart!
Try your best, think it through!”
“Okay Hiki, but just one more clue!”
Hiki pointed, to the sky,
Where stars and planets were rushing by.
A chobot cried, “It’s the moon!”
Another said, “Silly guy, what a buffoon!”
“No, he is correct,” Hiki said.
“Is she nuts?!” “Did she bump her head?!”
Hiki clicked her fingers,
And the chobots no longer lingered
On the planet they called home
Coz now they where free to roam.
On the big, silver eye
Of the huge, space and sky.
“Oh my god,” They all screamed
Hiki Chuckled, then she beamed.
“Let’s start the party! Have some fun!”
It started raining, for everyone.
Suddenly the moon went blue,
“Oi, Hiki! What did you do?!”
“Come on guys! Loosen up!
It’s just a pary! Let’s pump it up!”
So all the chobots had some fun,
Until finally the day was done.
One by one, they left the moon,
Glad that there was never, any doom.
The moon party went down on chobots history,
But how Hiki got there was always a mystery.

Wow :) It's so cool! Thank you Yumega for the great poem!

Wednesday, January 6, 2010

Can't log on?

Attention chobots!

As you should have noticed, yesterday we had some updates and Chobots were unavailable for a brief period.
I've been told that after the updates some of you have problems with logging.
So I'm here to help you :)
If Chobots doesn't work for you all you have to do is refresh the DNS cache :
  1. Click "Start"
  2. Click "Run"
  3. Type "ipconfig /flushdns" (without quotation :)
  4. Press "Enter"
  5. Close the window
  6. Then clear your browser's cache
  7. Restart the browser and enjoy playing Chobots :)

This should help :) Comment here if you face any problems.
I'll be glad to help!
