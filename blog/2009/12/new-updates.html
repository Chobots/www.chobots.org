New updates!

Yeap, you are not dreaming! There are new updates, lots of updates!!!!

Oki-doki, let's start with 2 new games.

The first one is called Chopix Adventure. You can find it in the Space Port Square between Cinema and Space Racing. It is very cool, u should try it!!


The Second game is called Chobots Racer. You can find it in the Space Quay location, near the Garbage Collector.

Though i am really noobie at this game, it is very exciting and funny!


So, those are 2 new games. What else have we got new ?

There is one more catalog (near music instruments catalog). I can't tell you what will you be able to buy there in the future, but you can try to guess :)

Also there is a pedestal under the new catalog. If you want your chobot to be shown there just click on the button below.

In the end i want to tell you about one more cool update! Now for the win in each game in chobots u get twice as much money. Isn't that cool ???
