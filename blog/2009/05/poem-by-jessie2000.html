Poem by Jessie2000!





Hello bloggers!
Jessie2000 wrote an awesome poem about me ^_^




Our Hikikomori

A friend to all is Hikikomori
A "party girl", so what's her story?

She arrived on Chobots not so long ago
With a great big smile and a loud hello

She soon teamed up with our old friend Moonkee
To have a great time and make everyday a fun day

She seems never to tire and stays up late all the time
Working hard on the blog
Or trying to get us to stand in a line :)

So if you see her say Thank you
And be glad she has come
As our Chobot world
Wouldn't be this much fun.
Thank you Jessie!!! :)
