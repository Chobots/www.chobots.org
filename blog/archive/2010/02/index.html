Chobots Spring Blogs

Hey Guys :)

Time to announce the winners for the Spring Blog Contest!!

Blog by Smartdetective

Blog By Kiwii

Blog By Im_so_emo_9

Blog By Gelu_cool

Nice Blogs, well done guys you win the Feather :)

Some tips for the Bloggers out there!!
  • Post regularly, like everyday if possible, there's always something happening in Chobots to post about ^_^
  • Always post the updates and changes happening in Chobots.
  • Make them interesting to read and pleasing to the eye.
  • Check spellings and keep your blog looking neat, if a picture doesn't fit properly then try again and see if you can fix it.
  • Don't have a ton of trackers at the top of your blog, it makes your blog very laggy and your reader may leave before it's finished loading.
  • If you have rain on your blog, check to see that it doesn't interfere with people commenting, some of your blogs I tried to follow and couldn't because the rain stopped me from clicking on your followers.
  • If you want to become an Agent of Chobots then having a blog that's helpful and informative and regularly updated shows us that you take a good interest and are committed to the game.

Keep Yourselves Safe!!!

Hey Guys :)

This post if for you all of you to read, I want you to take notice of what it says
and the information that is in it!!
It will help you to keep your Chobot safe.




You should all be aware that if anyone offers to make you a Citizen/Agent/Mod
and all they need is your password - It ISN'T going to happen, what will happen
if you give it to them is this, They will take control of your Chobot, change the pass,
sell your clothes, delete your friends and spend all your bugs!!
These are Scammers and we don't want them in our Chobot world, if someone
offers to do this for you, Report them and add them to your ignore list!!

Another way they may try to fool you is by FAKE email addresses or Skype
accounts, don't be fooled, Chobots has a Skype account which is there to help
with any problems you may have and only that, The name of it is
Chobots, it is based in Ukraine and is the ONLY Official Chobots Skype
I would suggest that you don't try to find any Mods personal Skype accounts as
there are a lot out there that are using our names which are not us.
If a Mod wants you to have their Skype name, they would have posted it
or gave it to you personally.

The only email for Chobots support is support@chobots.com
Some Mods do allow you to email them for competitions or such and if they do
then they post their email addresses on their blogs. You can find a list of Mods
blogs at the side of this blog.

Lastly, "sharing" accounts is not allowed, to share an account you will have had to
give out your password and this is against the rules, DO NOT allow others to go
on your account just to buy you Citizenship either, you could be scammed and
someone elses parents should not be paying for you to be a Citizen.

Treat others how you wish to be treated, play safe and be happy :)
