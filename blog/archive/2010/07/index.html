This document was created using the Elder Futhark Runic alphabet. This alphabet was used between 100CE and 700CE, and only contained 24 runes. There was no rune (letter) "C" in its alphabet, so you would have had to use the "K" to spell Chobots.

Congrats and Good Job Everyone!!  Dannyotoy was the first to decode the message. It reads: "Who will figure this out?"
