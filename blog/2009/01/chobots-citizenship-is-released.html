Chobots Citizenship is Released!

It finally happened! Chobots can become Citizens.


First of all, citizens receive a badge-passport to your player card! This  tells everybody that you are a big fan of Chobots and that you are an experienced player.


To get even more visual, citizens get bigger chat bubble in the public chat! 

But this is not all, folks! Now, citizens can buy exclusive suits for bugs you earned playing hard! And lots of new items will come soon!


And of course, there is exclusive magic stuff never seen before! It will shake your mind!



And finally, there are big houses for big parties! BTW, this one has 2 floors! And soon, there will be an option to design you home!

Ups... Did I forget? Citizens can also use graffiti! But don't get banned, we're watching over you and we know who draws what.

At the end, I just would like to remind you that Chobots never stop. Many new locations, cities, games and features are going to be available for everybody. And this will happen soon! 

Let the Cho be with you! ;)
