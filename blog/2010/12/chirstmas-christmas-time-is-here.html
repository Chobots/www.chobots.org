Chirstmas Christmas time is here!

Hey guys William here,

The sound of those bells is coming nearer! And it sounds like it’s time for a video contest!

So this week’s topic is “Christmas” :)


There will be a total of 5 winners!

Awards will be given out for:
• Best Music Video
• Best Story Video
• Most creative video
• Most factual
• Most festive

Yes, that’s right a total of 5 prizes.

When you submit your video please include in the title which category you’re going for:
E.g. “Christmas video by William (Most Festive)”

You can only submit one video in one category.
Please comment your video link in the comments to this post.
You have 1 week to complete your videos (19th December 2010)

Winners will be able to have their videos in the Cinema by Christmas!

Good luck, Keep rolling,
-William
