Attention All Bloggers!!

Hey Guys :)

I was thinking that we have this item, the Feather which was made for blog competitions but not very many of you you own one at the moment!!

So, from now on, once a month, I will make a post and ask you if you think you and your blog is worthy of being awarded the feather item and I will check them to see!!


To earn it you will be expected to post very regularly, every day would be great, especially any updates or changes that is happening in Chobots!
Party announcements and pictures are cool and any fun stuff or information that you think will be interesting to read!

There is always something going on in Chobots, so this should be easy, You could also make all sorts of competitions for "fun" and although you can't give the winners an item prize, you could show their player card on your blog or have a little party with your friends for them maybe!

It should also be well presented to the best of your abilities :)

If you think your blog already meets this criteria then don't hesitate to comment me a link to it now and I will check it out! I will follow and keep my eye on them for a while ;)

Wednesday, February 24, 2010

Chobots Spring Blogs

Hey Guys :)

Time to announce the winners for the Spring Blog Contest!!

Blog by Smartdetective

Blog By Kiwii

Blog By Im_so_emo_9

Blog By Gelu_cool

Nice Blogs, well done guys you win the Feather :)

Some tips for the Bloggers out there!!
  • Post regularly, like everyday if possible, there's always something happening in Chobots to post about ^_^
  • Always post the updates and changes happening in Chobots.
  • Make them interesting to read and pleasing to the eye.
  • Check spellings and keep your blog looking neat, if a picture doesn't fit properly then try again and see if you can fix it.
  • Don't have a ton of trackers at the top of your blog, it makes your blog very laggy and your reader may leave before it's finished loading.
  • If you have rain on your blog, check to see that it doesn't interfere with people commenting, some of your blogs I tried to follow and couldn't because the rain stopped me from clicking on your followers.
  • If you want to become an Agent of Chobots then having a blog that's helpful and informative and regularly updated shows us that you take a good interest and are committed to the game.
