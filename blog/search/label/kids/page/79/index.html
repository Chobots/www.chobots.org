Chobots Rules Poem!

Hello guys!

Jessie2000 wrote an awesome poem about chobots rules!

Chobots a family game
A place to have some fun
So here's some rules
We all should know
To make sure no harms done

"Dating" tut-tut, no-no
Please don't do that here
Remember it's a family game
For fun and friends and cheer

"Bad Words" Just can't be said
It's not the Chobot way
The Mods find out if you do something wrong
If said, your chat will soon be gone

"Spamming" for us to hear
Is really such a pain
Say once or twice and that will do
The worst I've heard
Is rain, rain, rain

"Passwords" A word that only you should know
Never share, Never ask
DON'T TELL YOUR PASS
If you do, I'm afraid
Your Chobot days
Will be over, oh so fast.
