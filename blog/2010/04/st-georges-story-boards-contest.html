St Georges Story Board Contest!!

Hey Guys :)

I have a new research contest for you all to take part in!!

St Georges Day is coming up soon, so I would like to do some research about this day and why it is celebrated etc!!

Instructions coming up PLEASE read them :)

Click the Story Board for full size and then right click and save to your computer!

Use this board to write your research on and you must make a small drawing to accompany it on the board!
The research must be in your own words, so no copy pasting and the same goes for the drawings, I want to see your own work not images from the net!!
Make sure your Chobot name is on the board too, plz don't change the size, just open the saved story board in paint etc and do your thing on it then save it again!

Comment working links to your finished boards at this post!!

Winners will receive the St Georges Flag :)

Have fun on Chobots today!

Jessie
