Let's Dress Up Vayerman Competition

Hey Guys

Well, you have dressed up Tina and I before
Now I think it's Vayermans turn to have a funky new look :))


He looks very cool in his Monk suit I know!!
But he has been wearing it a long time now huh??

As you can see I have made an attempt at a new outfit for him, lol :D
I would like you guys to do the same :))


Take a copy of this "Undressed" Vayerman and dress him up in whatever
you like, be as creative as you can and you may use anything you like
to make him look "Super Cool" I used a mixture of things :))

You have some days to do this
Comment a link to your finished designs here!!
Have fun :))

Winners will get Citizenship!!
